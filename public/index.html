Key Scientific Takeaway: On real tracking telemetry, LightGBM delivers the most reliable probabilistic calibration (lowest Brier 0.0312 and lowest ECE 0.0206). However, simple regularized logistic regression slightly edges out tree ensembles on raw discrimination ranking (ROC-AUC 0.716 vs 0.677, PR-AUC 0.074 vs 0.064). This honestly reflects sports-science findings (Carey et al. 2018, Impellizzeri et al. 2020): complex ML cannot extract certainty from small-sample, low-prevalence (~3%) athletic datasets.
